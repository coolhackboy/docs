## Model

Use `seedance-2.5` for text-to-video, first/last-frame generation, and multimodal reference-to-video.

## Input modes

- Omit media fields for text-to-video.
- Use `image_urls` with one starting frame and an optional ending frame.
- Use `reference_image_urls`, `reference_video_urls`, and `reference_audio_urls` for multimodal references. Mention them in the prompt as `@Image1`, `@Video1`, or `@Audio1`.

## Important notes

- `prompt`, `duration`, and `resolution` are required.
- `duration` accepts integer values from 4 to 30 seconds.
- `resolution` accepts `480p`, `720p`, or `1080p`.
- `image_urls` accepts at most two images, cannot be combined with reference media, and uses `aspect_ratio: auto`.
- Reference mode accepts up to 30 images, 10 videos, and 10 audio files, with at most 50 files in total.
- Audio references require at least one reference image or video.
- `aspect_ratio` accepts `auto`, `21:9`, `16:9`, `4:3`, `1:1`, `3:4`, or `9:16`.
- All media URLs must be publicly accessible and directly downloadable.
