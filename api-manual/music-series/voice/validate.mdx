---
title: "Validate Suno Voice"
description: "Extract a verification phrase from a voice sample"
icon: "microphone"
openapi: "/api-manual/music-series/voice/validate.json POST /api/generate/submit"
---

Submit a publicly accessible recording with `vocal_start_s` and `vocal_end_s` selecting a clean vocal segment. The completed result contains `validate_info`; use the returned `task_id` for [Generate Voice](/api-manual/music-series/voice/generate) or [Regenerate Phrase](/api-manual/music-series/voice/regenerate).
