---
title: "Advanced Stem Split"
description: "Extract or remove a selected vocal or instrument stem"
icon: "sliders"
openapi: "/api-manual/music-series/vocal-remover/stem-split-advanced.json POST /api/generate/submit"
---

Provide exactly one audio source: either `task_id` plus `audio_id`, or `upload_url`. `stem_name` is required. The completed result returns `origin_data[]`; each item can contain `extract.audio_url` and `remove.audio_url`.

```json
{"model":"stem-split-advanced","input":{"upload_url":"https://example.com/song.mp3","stem_name":"guitar"}}
```
